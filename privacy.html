Relistr — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Summary: Relistr does not collect your data. It runs entirely in your own browser, has no backend, and sends nothing to us or to any third party.

What Relistr does. When you click the Relistr button on one of your own Poshmark listing pages, Relistr reads that listing (title, description, price, size, brand, condition, category, and photo URLs), opens the Facebook Marketplace create-listing form in a new tab in your browser, and fills the fields it can map. It also downloads the photos of that listing from Poshmark's image servers so it can place them into the Facebook form. You then review the form and press Publish yourself. Relistr never submits the form, never publishes anything, and never modifies your Poshmark listing.

What data leaves your browser. Nothing is sent to Relistr or its developer. Relistr operates no server and has no account system. The only outbound network requests Relistr makes are to Poshmark's own website and image CDN, solely to download the photos of the listing you chose to crosslist. Those requests are made without your login credentials (credentials are omitted).

What is stored, and where. Relistr stores a small amount of data using chrome.storage.local, which lives only on your own computer inside your browser profile: the listing currently being crosslisted (a temporary draft), a record of which fields the Facebook form could not fill, and a short append-only log of your most recent crosslists (the popup shows the last five). This data never leaves your machine and is not accessible to the developer. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension.

What Relistr never accesses. Relistr has no login for Poshmark or Facebook and never reads or stores your passwords, cookies, or authentication tokens. It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or any third-party services. It does not access any website other than Poshmark and Facebook Marketplace.

Permissions. Relistr requests only the permissions it needs to do the above: activeTab and scripting to read your listing and fill the Facebook form when you click the button, storage for the local records described above, and host access to poshmark.com, www.facebook.com, and Poshmark's image CDN.

Children. Relistr is a tool for online resellers and is not directed at children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
